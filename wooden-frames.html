Wooden Frames

Warm grains, honest profiles, and finishes that sit naturally in Indian homes.

The Material

Wood that holds a photograph still.

Our wooden line is for portraits, family groups, and rooms that want warmth without heaviness. We work with teak, oak, walnut-toned, natural pine, and painted woods — in classic, gallery, and slim profiles.

Every piece can be made to a standard size or cut for a custom print. Mounts, glass, and hanging hardware are specified with the image, not as an afterthought.
