Custom Framing

Have a Memory
in Mind?

From one special photograph to an entire memory wall, we help you create a framing solution that feels uniquely yours.

Made to the image

Nothing off the shelf has to be the last word.

Custom framing is for odd sizes, heirloom prints, certificates, needlework, collages, and walls that need a plan rather than a single purchase. You bring the photograph or the idea. We specify material, profile, mount, glazing, and hanging.

Visit the studio on RM Colony Main Road, or send a note with dimensions and a picture of the space. We will reply with a considered recommendation — not a generic quote.

How it works

Four conversations. One finished piece.

01

Share the moment

Photograph, print, file, or a wall you want to compose. Tell us who it is for.

02

Choose the hold

Wood, acrylic, metal, canvas, or a mixed arrangement for a memory wall.

03

Refine the details

Size, mount colour, glass, and finish are agreed before we cut.

04

Collect or receive

We finish, inspect, and pack the work so it is ready to hang.
