Privacy Policy

How Four Frames treats the information you share with the studio.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Four Frames (“we”, “our”, “the studio”) operates a static website and a physical framing studio at HB 37, RM Colony Main Road, Dindigul - 624 001, Tamil Nadu, India. This policy explains what we collect when you enquire, visit, or commission work, and how we use it.

Information we may collect

When you write to us, visit the studio, or place an order, we may hold:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Delivery or visit details
  • Photographs, files, and notes you provide for framing
  • Preferences about size, material, and finish

This website is static. Forms on these pages do not send data to a server by themselves. Anything you submit in the browser stays on your device unless you also contact us in person, by phone, or by another channel you choose.

How we use information

We use your details only to respond to enquiries, complete framing work, arrange collection or delivery, and improve our service. We do not sell personal information. We do not use your family photographs for marketing without your clear permission.

Photographs and files

Images you share are treated as private. We keep them for as long as needed to complete the work and resolve any finishing questions, then delete or return them if you ask. Please keep your own copy of original files and prints.

Third-party services

These pages may load fonts, scripts, maps, and images from third-party networks (for example Google Fonts, map embeds, and image hosts). Those providers may process technical data such as IP address according to their own policies.

Cookies

We do not set advertising cookies. Embedded maps or analytics you add later may set their own cookies; we will update this page if that changes.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold, request a correction, or ask us to delete files and contact details that are no longer needed for an order. Visit the studio or write through the contact page.

Children

We frame family photographs, including of children, only when a parent or guardian commissions the work. We do not knowingly collect children’s data through this website.
