The Material

A photograph that appears to float.

Acrylic is for rooms that prefer light to ornament. We polish edges, control thickness, and offer clear, frosted, magnetic, and block designs for desks and walls.

It is a favourite for gifting, office portraits, and contemporary living rooms where a heavy moulding would feel out of place.
